Why my minesweeper robot kept failing

October 27, 2026

While building my Mine Sweeper Robot, most of my problems were not about the search algorithm. They were about power.

The symptoms

I powered the robot with AA batteries. When the motors started or stalled, they pulled a sudden spike of current, called stall current. AA batteries have high internal resistance, so the voltage sagged under that load and the robot behaved unreliably.

Trying to fix it in code

I tried to solve the problem purely in software first. It helped less than I hoped, because the cause was electrical, not logical.

The fix

I bought 18650 Li-ion batteries, which handle current spikes much better. That simple hardware change did more than my code changes.

What I took from it

Over five documented trials, I learned to find the root cause of each failure before changing anything. Sometimes the simplest fix is the right one.
