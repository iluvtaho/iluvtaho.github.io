To build or not to build?

I'm Jared, an aspiring mechatronics engineer based in Doha. I build embedded systems, robots, and machines from scratch, then document what breaks and why. So far that includes an autonomous mine-sweeping robot, a MicroSD-based personal cloud device, and a commissioned temperature monitoring system.

I learn by iterating. My robot took five documented trials to work, and the biggest lesson was that some problems are electrical, not logical. Right now I'm building OctoCam, a digital camera from scratch, and an automated mini drill feed machine.

Outside engineering, I manage a team of student researchers at Big Appletunities and coach U10 basketball.
