Temperature Monitoring Box

A commissioned system that detects someone entering a room, measures the temperature, and says whether to raise or lower it.

Board
Arduino Uno R3
Sensors
HC-SR501 PIR, DHT11
Output
16x2 I2C LCD, buzzer, SG90 servo
Dates
Sep to Oct 2026

How it works

  1. The PIR sensor detects someone entering.
  2. A servo sweeps the DHT11 temperature sensor left to right three times.
  3. The Arduino averages the three readings.
  4. The LCD shows the average and a COLD, WARM, or HOT verdict, and the buzzer sounds.

Design decisions

  • I removed the relay in the client's original materials because the Uno's 5V pin is within every component's ratings.
  • I designed a 3D-printed case instead of using a plastic box, for a custom fit.
  • The servo shares the Uno's 5V pin. A future version could give it a dedicated 5V supply.
