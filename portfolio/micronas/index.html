Why

Home NAS devices often cost $200 to $500 and need dedicated hard drives. MicroNAS aims to give private, remote-accessible storage at a fraction of the cost and size.

What I built

  • Designed and 3D modeled the enclosure for printing
  • Built and soldered the full circuit around the ESP32
  • Wrote the ESP32 code that reads and writes MicroSD cards
  • Connected the ESP32 to the web interface
