Mine Sweeper Robot

An autonomous robot that searches a minefield with a metal detector, built to apply MIT OpenCourseWare 2.12 Introduction to Robotics.

Board
NodeMCU-32S (ESP32)
Sensor
Inductive proximity switch
Power
18650 Li-ion (originally AA)
Trials
5
Fastest clear
1 min 53 s, 90 x 120 cm field

Final build, tested on a 90 x 120 cm minefield

How it works

The robot drives across a field and uses an inductive proximity switch to detect metal. A search algorithm plans its path, aiming for the shortest time to clear the whole field.

The power problem

With AA batteries the robot kept failing because of stall-current spikes, voltage sag, and battery internal resistance. I tried to fix it in code first, but the real fix was hardware: 18650 Li-ion cells.

What I learned

Five documented trials taught me to trace each failure to its root cause before changing anything, and that some problems are easiest to solve with a simple hardware change.
